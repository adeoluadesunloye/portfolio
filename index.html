Origin story

No cape, just footwork

The short version of how I got here, and what I'm looking for next.

I started in Lagos, on foot.

My first jobs were not marketing jobs so much as building jobs: two marketplace apps that existed on paper and needed merchants before they could have a single customer. So I mapped every business in a district on Google Maps, split the map into territories, and walked them. I pitched, I signed, I onboarded, and I went back when the first orders came in. That taught me the thing I still believe: a strategy is only as good as the number of doors it gets you through, and a sale is only finished when the customer comes back.

Eight years on, I have run the whole flywheel more than once, from zero. At 201Stores I generated £300k+ in revenue at a 60% close rate and cut merchant churn by 40% by fixing what users complained about; at CoupinApp I grew merchants on the platform 48% and active deals 4.5 times, for 70% revenue growth. At Prinvest 2022 I sold out a national property conference as sales lead, one speaker and one exhibitor at a time. At New Vision Hospitality, three London restaurant and bar venues, a social and PR push lifted revenue by 35% and private bookings by 60%, and a rebuilt website lifted direct bookings by 30%.

Around those roles I deliberately worked the other side of the counter: night reception at Beds and Bars and at Frasers Hospitality’s five-star Fraser Suites in London, running Opera Cloud, night audits and Booking.com enquiries. That was the point of the whole mix: SaaS marketplaces, trade, education, events, hospitality and charity, each one chosen so I would understand a different market from the inside and could turn what its customers actually do into insight for the next one. In London I took a Master's in Strategic Marketing Management at the University of Greenwich and won the GreHacks challenge with Ambitious About Autism. Straight after it, at SourceAZ, I built the brand, the website and a £150k+ new business pipeline from nothing as Marketing Business Development Manager, converted 64% of leads to opportunities and took a three-person team to 20% above KPI. Then at EkoHacks, a developer education startup, I took revenue from £0 to £10,000 a year in recurring revenue, with 30 paying customers.

Today I run Gleamcave, a behavioural-science-led marketing consultancy for experience-led businesses that grew out of the Lagos agency I started in 2021 and that I took from an idea to launch, where that cross-industry read on customer behaviour is the product. I'm looking for a full-time growth, business development or marketing role where the targets are real and the product deserves to be found. I don't stop at awareness. Give me the lead list and I'll give you the conversions and the repeat customers. I work well in a team, I'm comfortable with a quota, and I don't mind the phone.

“Know thyself. Know the customer. Innovate.”
